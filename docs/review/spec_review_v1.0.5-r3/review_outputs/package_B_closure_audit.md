E. Overall assessment
On the repository evidence, the closure record is largely well supported. The archives and adoption-record histories check out, the statistical and scanner code is unchanged, the self-attested safeguards are labelled as such, the simulations are scoped to the procedure, and the AI reviews are not presented as human validation. G02, G03 and G10 are implemented as the owner decided; the G10 calibration passes its stated criterion.

The main open issue is G08. The record itself says the owner's wording ("seven specified pqscan arms") may differ from the Part 1 set that was implemented, yet G08 is marked CLOSED. Because Part 1's outcome depends on that set, it should be confirmed before any H4 evaluation.

The other findings are minor clarity or evidence gaps (TB02–TB12). None of them shows a changed hypothesis, threshold or estimand. This audit does not validate the study and says nothing about whether its hypotheses hold.
